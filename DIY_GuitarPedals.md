# Guitar Pedal Building Resouces

## books:
- [Electronic Projects for Musicians](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/348604.Electronic_Projects_for_Musicians) by Craig Anderton
  - [pdf](http://www.apo33.org/pub/PDF/electro_circuit_bending_books/Electronic%20Projects%20)
  - archive.org: https://archive.org/details/electronicprojec0000ande


## Guitar pedal PCBs:
- [PedalPCB](https://www.pedalpcb.com/) - great PCBs, decent prices
- [GuitarPCB](https://www.guitarpcb.com/) - 1-person shop, good deals
- [MadBeanPedals](https://www.madbeanpedals.com/) - another 1-person shop, great info and forum 
  - Madbean has a cool pdf about understanding PCB layouts and schematics: [Understanding PCB Layouts](https://www.madbeanpedals.com/tutorials/downloads/MBP_Understanding_Layouts.pdf)


## Guitar pedal Parts:
- [Tayda](https://www.taydaelectronics.com/) 
  - a great source for guitar pedal parts and kits, enclosures, etc.
  - decent quality, great prices
  - shipping from Thailand takes a little longer
- [Love my Switches](https://lovemyswitches.com/)
    - a great small business with quick shipping for guitar pedal parts: electronics, jacks, knobs, enclosures, etc. 
- [Stomp Box Parts](https://www.stompboxparts.com/) 
    - another small business with quick shipping for guitar pedal parts: electronics, jacks, knobs, enclosures, etc.
